WHY `--forked`
  A plain full run and 573-test shards are both SIGKILLed (exit 137, OOM) at ~37% / ~50% in
  4 GiB. `pytest --forked` (one process per test, memory freed after each) is the only way
  the full suite completes in 4 GiB. The trade-off: forking ISOLATES each test, so any test
  that depends on a previous test's side effects fails under it (see the 2 isolation
  artifacts below). That is a property of `--forked`, not of Python 3.12.
